Privacy notice

Sterling Row Barbers collects only what we need to hold your chair and reply to you. This notice explains that in plain language. Last updated 24 September 2026.

What we collect

When you book, you give us your name, email, phone number, chosen service, barber, date, time, offer code, and any note you type. The contact form uses your name, email, and message, and opens your own email app so you can send it. We do not run a separate account system and we do not ask for payment card details on this website.

How it is used

Booking details are used to show your confirmation and to build a calendar event for Google Calendar or an .ics file for Apple Calendar and similar apps. A copy of the latest booking is stored in your browser’s session storage so the confirmation can be rebuilt if you stay on the page. It is not sent to a Sterling Row server.

Who else sees it

If you choose Google Calendar, Google receives the event details because you open their calendar link. If you download the .ics file, that file stays on your device until you add it to a calendar app. Maps on the contact page are provided by Google.

How long we keep it

Session storage clears when you close the browser tab’s session. If you email or call us, we keep the correspondence for as long as we need it to manage your appointment and then delete it in the ordinary course of business.

Your choices

You can book without an offer code. You can skip the calendar buttons. To ask what we hold, or to correct a booking, phone 021 555 0148 or email book@sterlingrow.example.
